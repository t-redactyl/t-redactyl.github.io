Other work

Books

I have co-authored two books: "The Hitchhiker's Guide to ggplot2" and "The Hitchhiker's Guide to plotnine". These books will help you to build beautiful plots in R and Python using powerful packages based on the Grammar of Graphics.

If you'd like easily to create highly customised plots, including replicating styles such as XKCD and fivethirtyeight, these books are for you.

Humble Data

I am an organiser of the Humble Data workshop, a community initiative to introduce people from underrepresented groups to data science. These are designed for people who have never coded before, and teach them the basics of Python, Jupyter, pandas and data visualisation.

We're looking for people to help us improve the workshops, as well as run their own. If you're interested in finding out more, please visit our website!
